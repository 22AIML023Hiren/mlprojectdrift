Quadrant 1 • Problem, Motivation and Impact
Foundations & Scope

The Core Problem

Standard supervised ML relies on the i.i.d. assumption (Ptrain(X,Y) = Ptest(X,Y)). In high-stakes financial domains (fraud detection, credit scoring), this assumption fails due to evolving attacker tactics, macroeconomic swings, and temporal shifts. High-capacity models make catastrophic, overconfident errors on shifted data without self-awareness of their unreliability.

Limitations of Current Practice

  • Poor Calibration Under Shift: Raw softmax or tree leaf probabilities are uncalibrated out-of-distribution (OOD), yielding high confidence on incorrect guesses.
  • Noise vs. Novelty Conflation: Conventional models fail to separate aleatoric noise (feature overlap) from epistemic uncertainty (novel distribution spaces).
  • OOD ≠ Decision Risk: Distance-based OOD detectors flag unfamiliar points regardless of whether the prediction itself is safely separable.

Beneficiaries

Fraud Analysts & Risk Teams: Workload reduced by auto-clearing safe transactions and routing ambiguous ones to triage.
Consumers: Minimized false card declines and zero-day fraud losses.

Scientific & Practical Impact

Formalizes selective classification for tabular data, enabling a risk-aware human-in-the-loop paradigm that slashes shift-induced fraud losses while automating >85% of volume.

Controlled Shift Injection Protocol

Split: Chronological 70% Train (199k), 15% Calib/Val (42k), 15% ID Test (42k).
Shifts: (1) Covariate Shift: Gaussian jitter on key features V1–V4; (2) Subpopulation Shift: High-amount tail strata (>90th pct); (3) Prevalence Shift: Skew fraud rate 0.17% → 2.5%; (4) Temporal Drift: Final 8-hr slice. Ethics: Anonymized PCA, zero PII.

Core Algorithms & Baselines

  • Predictors: LightGBM, Random Forest, Logistic Regression, Tabular Deep Ensemble.
  • Uncertainty Estimators: (a) Calibration: Isotonic Regression & Platt Scaling; (b) Ensemble Disagreement: Inter-tree variance Uens = (1/M)Σ(p̂m - p̄)2; (c) Conformal Prediction: Non-conformity sets with coverage guarantee 1 - α.
  • Baselines: (1) Vanilla Softmax with static 0.5 threshold; (2) Random deferral; (3) Isolation Forest OOD filter.

Key Proposed Contributions (Specific & Distinct)

  • C1. Tabular Shift Benchmark Suite: Standardized multi-axis synthetic & temporal shift generation harness for imbalanced tabular benchmarks.
  • C2. Comparative Risk Estimator Bake-off: First direct empirical comparison of calibration vs. ensemble variance vs. conformal bounds under tabular shift.
  • C3. Cost-Sensitive Selective Policy: Risk-Coverage optimization routing ambiguous decisions to human analysts while preserving ≥85% automated clearance.
  • C4. Shift Detection vs. Decision Risk Disentanglement: Clarifying that OOD feature status does not imply misclassification risk, and vice versa.
